## Data and editorial standards

All public records are restricted by Supabase Row Level Security and by status and editorial approval.
The verified initial dish is Boudie’s own vermicelli milk pudding recipe.
No untested fusion ideas, invented venues, unlicensed imagery, unreviewed creator spotlights or fabricated badges may be portrayed as real recommendations.
A newly accessible page is not evidence that the directory has complete content. Empty states must remain transparent while the editorial database grows.

## Accounts

Public readers and contributors use /account/, not /editorial-desk/.
Editors and administrators alone receive Editorial Desk application authorization.
Passport records are scoped to the authenticated owner in Supabase.
The static HTML shell of /editorial-desk/ is not itself confidential; a separate Cloudflare Access/edge 403 gate is still an enhancement, not currently declared complete.

## Acceptance

- Bilingual page/SEO and public navigation tests pass.
- Real-browser mobile layout and role-based auth scenarios are checked after deployment.
- Free newsletter opt-in remains separate from launch updates.
- No paid content, external collaborator branding, or generated photographs are introduced.
